<!--
  BASICS.TXT: the starter elements. Paint with them, read them, change them.

  Every element starts with ## and a name (lowercase letters, numbers and
  dashes). Under the name, any of these lines:

    color: orange, #e2c290        one or more colors: names, or # codes
    moves: falls                  stays, falls (piles up like sand),
                                  flows (spreads like water), or
                                  rises (floats up like smoke)
    weight: 3                     heavier things sink through lighter ones
                                  (if you leave it out: falls 3, flows 2, rises 1)
    lasts: 40                     disappears after 40 frames (60 frames is
                                  about one second), or lasts: 20-60
    then becomes: smoke           ...or turns into smoke when its time is up

    becomes fire when touching lava (20%)   changes ITSELF
    turns water into steam (20%)            changes what it TOUCHES
    turns empty into smoke (5%)             "empty" means blank space

  The % is the chance it happens each frame. Leave it off for 100%.

  Lines inside these comment marks are notes. The sandbox never reads them.
-->

## sand
<!-- The simplest kind of element: some colors and a way to move. -->
color: #e2c290, #d4b483, #c9a96e
moves: falls

## water
color: #2f7fd8, #3b8de6
moves: flows

## stone
color: #7d7d7d, #6e6e6e, #8a8a8a
moves: stays

## wood
color: #8b5a2b, #7a4a22
moves: stays
becomes fire when touching fire (15%)
becomes fire when touching lava (30%)

## fire
<!-- Fire is lighter than everything, so it rises fast, and it doesn't last. -->
color: orangered, #ff8c00, #ffc300
moves: rises
weight: 0.5
lasts: 8-30
becomes steam when touching water
turns empty into smoke (1%)

## smoke
color: #6b6b6b, #808080, #5a5a5a
moves: rises
lasts: 30-90

## steam
<!-- When steam's time is up, it turns back into water: rain! -->
color: #dfe9f0, #c8d6e0
moves: rises
weight: 0.8
lasts: 120-300
then becomes: water

## lava
<!-- Heavier than water, so it sinks under it. -->
color: orangered, #e03a00, #ff6a00
moves: flows
weight: 2.5
turns water into steam (40%)
turns ice into water (50%)
becomes stone when touching water (15%)

## oil
<!-- Lighter than water, so it floats on top. And it burns. -->
color: #4a3214, #5c4019
moves: flows
weight: 1
becomes fire when touching fire (40%)
becomes fire when touching lava (50%)

## seed
color: #c8a165, sienna
moves: falls
becomes plant when touching water (5%)

## plant
<!-- Plants drink the water next to them, and grow into it. -->
color: seagreen, mediumseagreen, forestgreen
moves: stays
turns water into plant (2%)
becomes fire when touching fire (25%)
becomes fire when touching lava (50%)

## ice
color: #bfe6ff, #a8dcff
moves: stays
turns water into ice (0.1%)
becomes water when touching fire (40%)
becomes water when touching steam (2%)
