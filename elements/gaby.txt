## mercury
color: silver, #C0C0C0
moves: flows
weight: 3
becomes mercury-vapor when touching fire (10%)
becomes mercury-vapor when touching lava (80%)
becomes mercury-vapor when touching steam (1%)

## mercury-vapor
color: #c9cdd1, #e0e3e6, #b5bbc2
moves: rises
weight: 1
becomes mercury when touching water (0.5%)
becomes mercury when touching ice (5%)
becomes mercury when touching stone (0.1%)
